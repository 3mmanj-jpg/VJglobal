THE WHOLESALE GUIDE · BY VERGINO S. JULES

From Zero to Wholesale Hero

The Complete Step-by-Step Guide to Selling Brand-Name Products with Amazon FBA and Walmart WFS.

Are you ready to start a real online business using products people already trust?

Instead of creating a product from scratch, wholesale selling allows you to work with established brands and products that customers already buy. This guide walks you through the foundations of building a wholesale e-commerce business.

What You'll Learn Inside:
  • How to set up your business and prepare your documents.
  • How to open and understand Amazon and Walmart seller accounts.
  • How to research brand-name products and wholesale opportunities.
  • How to find and evaluate wholesale distributors.
  • How to understand product costs, fees, and profit margins.
  • How Amazon FBA and Walmart WFS fulfillment work.

THE LEARNING ROADMAP

Your 7-Day Wholesale Journey

A structured introduction to the main steps involved in starting a wholesale e-commerce business.

Day 1 · Wholesale Fundamentals

Understand wholesale, retail arbitrage, private label, marketplace basics, and the business model.

Day 2 · Business Setup

Learn about business readiness, common documents, and seller-account applications.

Day 3 · Product Research

Explore product demand, competition, categories, brand research, and listing quality.

Day 4 · Suppliers

Learn how to approach brands and distributors, request information, and evaluate suppliers.

Day 5 · Profit Planning

Understand product costs, fees, shipping, margins, and inventory planning.

Day 6 · Prep & Fulfillment

Explore listing matching, packaging, labeling, shipment planning, FBA, and WFS.

Day 7 · Next Steps

Review your action plan, operations, account health, and next steps for your business.
