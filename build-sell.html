The program

Build knowledge before you buy inventory.

The seven-session class is designed to introduce wholesale concepts, marketplace basics, product research, supplier vetting, profit planning, and shipment preparation.

Participants remain responsible for their own account decisions, supplier verification, marketplace compliance, and business results.

Class dates, session length, time zone, tuition, payment process, and seat availability must be confirmed with the business before enrollment.

What the outline covers

  • Wholesale fundamentals and marketplace basics
  • Business readiness and seller-account application walkthroughs
  • Product, brand, and competition research
  • Supplier outreach and verification
  • Landed cost, fees, margins, and inventory planning
  • Listing matching, labeling, and shipment preparation
  • Operations, account health, action planning, and Q&A
Seven-session outline

A structured introduction

The final schedule and session length will be confirmed before enrollment.

Day 1 · Wholesale fundamentals

Wholesale vs. retail arbitrage and private label, key terms, marketplace basics, realistic costs and risks.

Day 2 · Business and seller accounts

Business readiness, commonly needed documents, application walkthroughs, account settings, and verification.

Day 3 · Product and brand research

Demand, competition, category restrictions, listing quality, pricing, and research workflows.

Day 4 · Finding and vetting suppliers

Approaching brands and distributors, requesting price lists and invoices, verifying legitimacy, and keeping records.

Day 5 · Profit and inventory planning

Landed cost, marketplace fees, shipping, prep costs, estimated margins, and cautious test orders.

Day 6 · Listing, prep, and fulfillment

Matching existing listings, packaging, labeling, shipment planning, and an overview of FBA and WFS.

Day 7 · Launch and next steps

Inventory, pricing, orders, returns, account health, customer-service basics, action plan, and Q&A.

Suggested format in the brief: one live Zoom session per day, with a teaching segment, practical example, participant questions, and a short action task. Materials are subject to finalization.
